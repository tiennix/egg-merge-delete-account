Egg Merge – Delete Account & Data

Game: Egg Merge · Developer: Geminix

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to tientv.dev@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Egg Merge – Delete my account.
  3. In the email, include the player name shown in the game, and ask us to delete your account and data.
  4. We confirm by email when your request is received, and complete the deletion within 30 days.
Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your account and any data linked to it.
  • Game progress: scores and high score, coins, boosters, missions and daily rewards.
  • Your in-game nickname and avatar choice.

Game data saved on your device can also be removed at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling the game.

What is kept

We do not keep any of your game data after the deletion is completed. Information processed by our advertising partner, Google AdMob, is handled under Google's Privacy Policy; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
